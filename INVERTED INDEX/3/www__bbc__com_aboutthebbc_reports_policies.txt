Policies and guidelines







BBC code of conduct
We let our staff know what we expect from them - and how our leaders will behave








Charities
In addition to our two corporate charities we also support a broad range of charities through weekly and monthly broadcast appeals








Child protection
Our policy and code of conduct are to ensure the safeguarding of children and young people working at or visiting the BBC








Distribution
How we govern the ways in which the BBC distributes its services and content of third-party platforms








Diversity and inclusion
How our workforce and our output reflects the UK as it is today








Editorial guidelines
All our programmes and content must adhere to our guidelines








Environmental sustainability

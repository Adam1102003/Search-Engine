Support for societies



Our portfolio of competitive and prestigious journals is hosted on our world-leading content platforms, which enable billions of articles to be downloaded each year.Our open access leadership enables us to address and solve challenges faced by the research community, guiding the sustainable path to OA, speeding up the transition to full OA, and helping to better address the problem of funding availability.Our exceptional author experience, from our submission system and web platform through to our production systems and collections tools, ensures that our partners are supported with the tools and services they need to exceed the expectations of their members and authors.Our commitment to helping global researchers achieve their desired community impact and connect their research with the policymakers who use it to make decisions that shape society around the world.Our focus on delivering positive change is aimed at being the foremost publisher—and curator—of peer-reviewed, up-to-date research, data, analysis and commentary that supports decision making by policymakers, business leaders and practitioners as they work to deliver real progress against urgent societal challenges.Our in-house strategic guidance and publication management delivers in-depth and ongoing feedback, based on sound and data-informed publication management and the perspective that comes from working across a large number of titles in the same discipline.Our investments in what we value means that we create and invest in systems and processes that make OA easy for authors, sustainable for partners, and equitable for society.Our outreach to China and emerging markets worldwide ensures that your research reaches the greatest possible audience through increased exposure and visibility of your journal with our extensive and established readership.

SeixasSummary:"This book concerns itself first and foremost with the question "How do students develop sophisticated historical understandings and how can teachers best encourage this process?" Recent developments in psychology, education, and historiography inform the debates that take place within this book. This four-part volume identifies the current issues and problems in history education, then works towards a deep and considered understanding of this evolving field ..."

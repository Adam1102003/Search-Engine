Welcome to the Representative Office of Standard Chartered Bank, Cambodia. We first established an office in Cambodia in 1953 and we were the first foreign bank to re-enter Cambodia after the country was re-opened in 1992. We now operate a Representative Office in Phnom Penh serving financial institutions in Cambodia and inbound clients across our footprint.

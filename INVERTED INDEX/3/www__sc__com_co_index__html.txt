Standard Chartered in Colombia
As a leading international cross-border bank, our unique footprint and diverse capabilities set us apart
Find out more

Standard Chartered has established a representative office in Bogota, which is registered with the Colombia Financial Superintendence. Our local representative office focuses on financial institutions and Latin American corporates with links to the Standard Chartered global network.
 As part of the Americas franchise, our Colombia representative office enables us to better serve our Americas client base by facilitating trade and investment flows between the Americas, Asia, Africa, the Middle East and Europe through offshore services.

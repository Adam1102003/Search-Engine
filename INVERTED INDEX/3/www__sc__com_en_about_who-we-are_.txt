Our purpose and values

We’re here for good
Our purpose is to drive commerce and prosperity through our unique diversity. We unlock sustainable growth across our markets to create long-term value for our clients, colleagues and communities. We believe we are better together, strive to always do the right thing and never settle.
As our brand promise makes clear, we’re here for good.




We’re taking a standWe believe that banking and finance can play a crucial role in creating a brighter future. Our long-term ambitions on climate, financial inclusion and globalisation help us make a difference across our footprint.

Accelerating zeroWe believe a just transition to net zero presents a huge opportunity for innovation and growth
Learn more

Lifting participationThrough partnerships and technology, we aim to expand the scale and reach of financial services
Learn more

Resetting globalisationWe advocate a new model of globalisation based on transparency and trust

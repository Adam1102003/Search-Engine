Standard Chartered in Iraq
A leading international bank focused on helping people and companies prosper across Europe, the Americas, Asia, Africa and the Middle East

Standard Chartered began domestic operations in Iraq in November 2013.During the challenging period in 2019, the bank did not close any account for high risk related issues or reject any potential client for high risk issues .
Our team consists of a team of cross-functional members who support trade finance, cash management, securities services, foreign exchange and risk management, capital generation, and corporate finance solutions.
Leading the way in Asia, Africa and the Middle East Standard Chartered is an international banking group that has operated for over 150 years in some of the world’s most dynamic markets. It earns around 90 percent of its income and profits in Asia, Africa and the Middle East .Standard Chartered is committed to building a sustainable business over the long term and is trusted worldwide for upholding high standards of corporate governance, social responsibility, environmental protection and employee diversity. The bank’s heritage and values are expressed in its brand promise, ‘Here for good’

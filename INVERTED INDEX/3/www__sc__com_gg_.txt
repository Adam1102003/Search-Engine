Business closure – Standard Chartered Trust (Guernsey) Limited(in liquidation)
We no longer have offices in Guernsey. If you’re interested in doing business with us in another European market, find out where we are at sc.com/locations.
If you want to find out more about the Bank, visit us at sc.com.
Following an application by Standard Chartered Bank Plc, on 19 May 2020, the Royal Court of Guernsey approved the Company’s application be wound up and that Alex Adam, Andy Wood and Stephen Browne of Deloitte LLP be appointed Joint Liquidators.
Any questions relating to the liquidation or data requests can be directed to the Joint Liquidators, by contacting then on SCTGliquidation@deloitte.co.uk or by writing to:
Standard Chartered Trust (Guernsey) Limited (in Liquidation)c/o Deloitte LLP,PO Box 137,Regency Court,Glategny Esplanade,St Peter Port,Guernsey,Channel Islands,GY1 3HW

Careers in China

We are one of the oldest international banks in China. Our first branch opened in Shanghai in 1858 and we‘ve had continuous operations since. We have outlets across nearly 30 coastal and inland cities, a testimony to our long-term commitment to China.
We also have the most diversified licenses in China. In 2018, we become the first international bank to be granted a domestic fund custody license by CSRC.

Careers in Greater Bay Area, China

GBA is a strategic focus for Standard Chartered and it acts as an important gateway connecting China to the world. Located at the heart of GBA in Guangzhou city, the Standard Chartered GBA Centre – launched in August 2021 -supports the dynamic and fastest growing opportunities in the new economy sectors in the region.

Relationship Managers in WRBThe work of Relationship Managers is to directly support our purpose – to drive commerce and prosperity – through the relationships built with new and existing clients; working closely with clients, taking the time to understand their financial needs and aspirations; to grow, manage and protect their wealth, with the right investment solutions.

Careers in Sustainable FinanceOur Sustainable Finance Solutions Team maintains our frameworks to help identify green and sustainable finance and transition finance opportunities. Careers in GBS IndiaStandard Chartered’s Global Business Services (GBS) began its operations in India in 2000 and is an owned subsidiary of Standard Chartered Bank UK PLC.

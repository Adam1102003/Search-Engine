						Understanding you					








												Banking in Jersey											

Standard Chartered in Jersey offer Private Banking services to customers with over US$2,000,000 to invest and Priority Banking services to customers with over US$100,000 to invest.






												Services											

Standard Chartered in Jersey offer a wide range of offshore products and services; which include Private and Priority Banking Services; Credit; Investment Opportunities and Treasury Solutions underpinned by the support of a qualified team of Relationship Managers and Client Advisors.






												Specialist offshore financial services											

Standard Chartered has an in-depth understanding of the markets in which it operates and a long-term commitment to them. Standard Chartered in Jersey has assets under management approximating US$6 billion and provides specialist offshore financial services on behalf of the Standard Chartered Group.








												Celebrating 45 years in Jersey											

Jersey is well known as one of the most developed and mature offshore financial centres in the world, and Standard Chartered is one of Jersey’s most well established institutions, with a presence spanning over 45 years.

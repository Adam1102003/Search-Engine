We’re proud of our inclusive culture – but know there’s more to do. That’s why we use data insights to understand our colleagues’ lived experiences and drive change. Explore our new Diversity, Equality and Inclusion Impact Report to see our progress.

Building our inclusive cultureOur strongly inclusive culture and our valued behaviours are essential building blocks for driving commerce and prosperity. We strive to be a great place to work and a great place to bank, while also supporting the communities where we operate.

Our focus areasWe believe people do their best work when they can draw on their lived experiences. Our five strategic focus areas help us support our colleagues to be their full selves at work.

DisabilityWe strive to be a disability confident organisation, removing barriers and increasing accessibility.
Sexual orientationWe look to provide support which enables colleagues to be themselves at work, whatever their identity.
GenderWe seek to engage all genders in our continued support and progress towards gender equality.


Nationality and ethnicityWe strive to build a workforce representative of our client base and footprint.
GenerationsGenerational experience helps us deliver products and services for people at different life stages.

Who
Karafesu Project Council


What

                                            1,667.301

                                            kilogram(s)

                                        


Where

                                        Japan
                                        (Nakatsu)



When

15 September 2019






The largest serving of fried chicken weighed 1,667.301 kg (3,675 lb 12 oz) and was achieved by Karafesu Project Council (Japan) in Nakatsu, Oita, Japan, on 15 September 2019.A total of 18 vendors from Nakatsu as well as neighboring cities participated in the attempt. The frying took place between 6AM and 10AM at the 12th annual Karaage Festival. 

Records change on a daily basis and are not immediately published online. For a full list of record titles, please use our Record Application Search. (You will need to register / login for access)
Comments below may relate to previous holders of this record.

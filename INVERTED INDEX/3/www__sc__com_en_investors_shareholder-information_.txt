Asset Reunification Programme with Georgeson
Standard Chartered PLC (the Company) has engaged Georgeson (a trading name of Computershare Investor Services PLC, the Company’s Registrar), to locate and unite gone-away and lost shareholders with their dividends and/ or shares.
Georgeson’s services are voluntary and as such you may contact our Registrar directly.
If you are contacted, it is important that you respond to Georgeson or Computershare in order to claim your dividend and/ or shares. In accordance with the Company’s Articles of Association, we intend to launch a programme to sell shares of those shareholders who we have been unable to contact for at least 12 years. The proceeds from the sale of shares and unclaimed cash entitlements would be used to support such good causes as the Company’s directors may decide.

How to stay informed
As a shareholder, we want to give you the choice about how we keep you up-to-date on your investment.
AGM and presentations
You’ll be invited to attend our Annual General Meeting where we answer investor questions. We’ll also keep in touch regularly with our institutional shareholders, including special presentations when we announce our financial results. If you can’t attend the AGM, you can register your proxy vote online.
Find information online
You can check our share prices, dividend details, news and stock exchange announcements and upcoming events on our website. With an online shareholder account, you can vote by proxy online, download webcasts and podcasts, sign up for email alerts and opt to receive shareholder communications electronically.
View our procedures for election of director  (中文)
Call us
If you need to speak to someone, you can call our registrars’ hotline or contact our Investor Relations team. We don’t call our shareholders directly to give any recommendations or advice, so please treat any approach that appears to come directly from us with caution.

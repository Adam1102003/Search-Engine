Sustainable Banking Report 2023
We reveal the potential for retail investors to combat climate change


Download latest report










Home > Campaigns > Sustainable banking report 2023 


A USD3.4 trillion opportunityfor climate investingRetail investor capital presents tremendous untapped potential for investments to flow into climate mitigation and adaptation themes

RenewablesClean energy to combat climate change and ensure energy security
Energy storageA new power system architecture to efficiently manage demand and supply
Resilient infrastructureAdapting the built environment to withstand weather changes


Top three marketsThese markets have the highest investment potential across Asia, Africa and the Middle East

China:USD2.33 trillionFocus themes:RenewablesEnergy storageFood systems
India:USD543 billionFocus themes:Energy efficiencyResilient infrastructureRenewables
South Korea:USD180 billionFocus themes:RenewablesBiodiversityEnergy storage


Tackling investing barriers

While interest in climate investing is growing, our survey of 1,800 investors reveals multiple technical and cognitive barriers holding them back. From accessibility to comparability, our report shows how collective industry action can help them overcome their barriers.

Home > Wealth and Retail Banking > Private Banking > Wealth solutions 

Whether you want to grow, protect or pass your wealth to the next generation, our customised solutions are designed to help you achieve your goals



Fixed income

A global team of fixed income specialists offering a suite of market access opportunities made possible from having long-established onshore operations in several emerging and frontier markets.
Receive actionable, market driven trade ideas generated by specialised investment strategists with unique expertise in selecting issuers across our global markets.






Equities

Access all major stock exchanges across the globe, and a spectrum of equity products covering listed equities, ETFs, listed warrants, equity structured notes and equity derivatives.
Our trading capabilities include Direct Market Access and algorithmic trading strategies across all major global exchanges.
Our central execution desk supports trading around the clock (24 hours x 5 days).






Foreign currency

Enjoy market leading breadth of product offering, speed of execution and quality of pricing, with best execution practice to obtain the most competitive prices for clients in most OTC FX Derivatives.
Our FX specialists can customise solutions ranging from simple foreign currency cash flow requirements to sophisticated tactical and strategic trading demands.






Structured products

Choose to limit downside risk by protecting the principal investment or take on more risk for potentially higher returns.
True open architecture platform offering a wide range of flow and non-flow payoffs and pricing from a broad range of best-in-class institutions.






Mutual funds

Our proprietary fund selection process screens a universe of over 113,000 funds to generate an independent list of 100-150 mutual funds for our clients.
Gain access to dedicated strategies across all asset classes from developed to frontier markets.






Discretionary portfolio

Our Signature programme is one of the first in our footprint to provide discretionary portfolio management with an open architecture.
Gain access to boutique third party investment managers, as well as our in-house portfolio managers, fund select and asset allocation expertise. Our range of portfolios offer diversity across asset class, geography, sector, and manager.






Lending

Optimise the value of your portfolio through our range of credit solutions.
Enhance your investment returns with Lombard lending across a variety of asset classes, and meet your liquidity needs with credit facilities available in all major global currencies.
Standard Chartered has a long history as a lender in our footprint markets. Our international presence positions us for cross border financing solutions across markets such as Hong Kong, Singapore, Dubai and London.
Our specialist team are experienced in structuring across a wide range of collateral including real estate and promoter shares.






Alternatives

Our dedicated alternative investment platform allows us to curate unique and exclusive investment opportunities for our clients. 
Gain access to established managers with proven and consistent track records across a wide range of alternative strategies that include private equity, private credit, real assets or hedge funds.






Wealth planning and insurance

We partner with market-leading insurance providers, allowing us to source reliable solutions best-suited to our clients’ profiles






Financial intermediaries

Specifically designed to foster collaboration to identify the best opportunities for your business growth. And our commitment extends to your clients’ custody and execution needs.
Access our private bank integrated platform supporting all key EAM processesReceive curated Chief Investment Office research and insightsMonitor portfolio and updates onlineAccess unique alternative investment opportunities
Full-suite solutions for independent financial advisors, multi-family offices and asset managers.

						Diversity and inclusion					

					Our employee resource groups				

						These groups are pivotal to helping us deliver our diversity and inclusion strategy					












Home > About us > Diversity and inclusion > Employee Resource Groups 

At Standard Chartered, when we say we promote diversity and an inclusive culture, we mean it. Our employee resource groups – or ERGs – help us do it.
We have groups for:
Gender equalityGenerationsPeople with disabilities, and carersOrigins, nationalities and ethnicitiesSC Pride: LGBT+ and allies



Our ERGs help drive us forward

Employee Resource Groups (ERGs) are voluntary, employee – led groups that foster a diverse, inclusive workplace aligned with organisational mission, values, goals, business practices and objectives.
Our ERGs help us to to:
Improve employee engagementEngage the organisation in a constructive dialogue on diversity and inclusionConnect individuals in the business Educate the workforce on issues affecting diverse groups and create greater cultural awarenessSupport local plans and the implementation of the D&I strategy



Catalysts for change
Employee Resource Groups (ERGs) can be a driving force to organisational change. They have the ability to underpin inclusion and transform culture. 
The initiatives ERGs hold are intended to educate, spread awareness and serve as a catalyst for innovative ways of overcoming barriers to equality.
ERGs are typically formed around specific dimensions of diversity such as gender, ethnicity, disability, age, sexual orientation, but they can also be based on other identities such as family and culture.
Our ERGs support a wide range of activities: 
Initiatives, campaigns & programmes that support awareness of D&IDevelop networking opportunities and skillsCareer development events, including seminars, leadership forums, buddy & mentoring programsSpeaker series’ with opportunities for discussions Cross-ERG events to share best practices and work towards common goalsSupporting community volunteeringSupporting recruitment effortsGenerating new ideas for products and servicesDeveloping and attracting new customers and clients

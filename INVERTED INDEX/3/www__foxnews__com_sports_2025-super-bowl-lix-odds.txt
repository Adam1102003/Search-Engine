And three months after the big game, they remain the favorites.The free agency period and NFL Draft moved the odds for a few teams, but now that those are in the past, the last rite of football spring passage was completed this week with the schedule reveal.   On May 15, the NFL released the schedule for the 272 regular season games and there were a couple of surprises.   For instance, 14 teams will have multiple Thursday games — which means shortened weeks of preparation — while six teams have zero Thursday night games.   Additionally, there are five regular season games being played outside the United States this year, with the Jacksonville Jaguars playing in two of them. Of course, teams already knew who they are going to play next season, but the scheduling of games can have a huge impact on the odds.   An early rough stretch might not be as important as a late one, and bettors are always looking for these arbitrage opportunities in futures markets. So, without further ado, They got their quarterback of the future in Caleb Williams and a potential star receiver in Rome Odunze, instantly making him one of, if not the best, No. 3 WR in the league. Chicago's offense now boasts Odunze with veterans DJ Moore and Keenan Allen, as well as D'Andre Swift, Cole Kmet, Gerald Everett and Williams at the helm." Atlanta is a team whose odds moved in a favorable direction after Day 1 of the NFL Draft, moving from +3000 to +2400. The Falcons added to their quarterback room, selecting Michael Penix Jr. with the No. 8 pick. Just last month, they signed four-time Pro Bowler Kirk Cousins.   On an episode of the NFL on FOX Podcast, FOX Sports NFL analyst Dave Helman said that, "in a vacuum," he understands the Penix pick."If you're comfortable with the medicals, you probably think this guy's great," Helman acknowledged. "The issue comes in the fact that the Atlanta Falcons gave Kirk Cousins $180 million two months ago!?"  FOX Sports NFC North reporter Carmen Vitali also chimed in on the Falcons' draft-night moves.   "Listen, the Atlanta Falcons are not the Green Bay Packers," she noted. "You can't get away in today's modern NFL by wasting a quarterback's rookie contract … team building doesn't work like that."A team whose Super Bowl futures got slightly longer is Dallas, as the Cowboys moved to +1500 from +1300 after the first night of the draft.

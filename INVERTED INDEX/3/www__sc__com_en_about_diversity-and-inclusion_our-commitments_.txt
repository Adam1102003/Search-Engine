						Diversity and inclusion					

					Our commitments				












Home > About us > Diversity and inclusion > Progress commitments 


Our commitment to equalityWe want to maintain a diverse workforce and find new ways to build on our inclusive culture. In support of this we have made a number of official commitments.

Women in Finance CharterIn 2016, we signed the UK HM Treasury Women in Finance Charter to support progress towards a fair industry.
Find out more

ILO CharterThe Global Business Disability Network Charter helps create equal opportunities for people with disabilities.
Find out more

UN PrinciplesWe agree to the Women’s Empowerment Principles, which offer guidance on how to promote gender equality.
Find out more

30% ClubWe joined the 30% Club to show our support for their mission to make women at least 30% of all boards.
Find out more



The Valuable 500We are one of the Valuable 500 companies committed to putting disability inclusion on their agenda.
Find out more

WEF Partnership for Racial JusticeA global coalition of organisation that aims to eradicate systemic racism in the workplace.
Find out more

UN Standards of ConductWe agree to the five Standards of Conduct to combat workplace discrimination against LGBTI+ people.
Find out more

UK Armed Forces CovenantThis initiative by the UK Government seeks to encourage fair treatment for the armed forces community.
Find out more

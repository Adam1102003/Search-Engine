eBooks licensing











Springer Nature offers the world’s most complete collection of eBooks spanning all subject areas, with an impressive portfolio of 260,000 eBook titles, including the Book Archives. A portfolio that is continuously growing through the dedicated work of editors publishing approximately 13,000 new peer-reviewed books annually. Making our eBooks an unparalleled resource to excel research and learning. Through collaboration with the library community a variety of licensing options have been developed and are available to you for this product.

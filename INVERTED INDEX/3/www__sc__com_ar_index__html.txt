Standard Chartered in Argentina
A leading international bank with a representative office in Argentina, we are focused on helping people and companies prosper across Europe, the US, Asia, Africa and the Middle East
Find out more

Standard Chartered has had a Representative Office in Argentina since 1996 (registered with the Argentina Central Bank). Our local representative office focuses on financial institutions and Latin American corporates with links to the Standard Chartered global network.
As part of the Americas franchise, our Argentina representative office enables us to better serve our Americas client base by facilitating trade and investment flows between the Americas, Asia, Africa, the Middle East and Europe through offshore services.

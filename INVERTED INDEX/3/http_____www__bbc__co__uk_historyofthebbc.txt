History of the BBC
Explore the history of the BBC since its foundation in 1922 to the present day





















Explore more than 100 years of the BBC’s history – click on our interactive timeline or investigate a topical anniversary, unpack the story of fascinating objects from our varied collections, or go in-depth via one of our long-form articles.
And tell us your memories of what the BBC means to you.


















Anniversaries

Director Ken Russell had already created some extraordinary television portrayals of composers Elgar, Prokofiev and Bartok before undertaking this experimental dramatic treatment of the life of Debussy in May 1965 for the arts series Monitor. Read more here.


















100 years of BBC Education 

BBC Education is marking 100 years of programme making this year. Dr. Steven Barclay, Policy Researcher at the Local Trust traces the evolution of schools broadcasting, taking you through the major programmes, series and moments.

















Objects of the BBC

The BBC Radiophonic Workshop was a creator of electronic and experimental music for BBC programmes such as the arts series Monitor. In its early years it used all manner of everyday objects to build up musical soundtracks. Read more about the instruments in the workshop's collection.

Voices of the BBC 

From life in the BBC as a telephone operator, to the pioneers of colour television and the BBC in the digital age, the BBC Oral History Collection has been recording the world of the corporation since 1972. Start your journey here through our themed collections.

















The road to restoration

Outside broadcast vans such as MCR21 (pictured), covered musical events such as the Proms, along with sport and even drama. Charles Runcie, former Head of Sport, BBC English Regions, explores how this icon of broadcasting has been brought back to life.
